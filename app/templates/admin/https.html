How guests reach it

Phones always find a sign-in page over plain HTTP. That's how captive-portal detection works on every brand. Once HTTPS is on, PassGate immediately sends them on to https://{{ s.tls_domain or 'your-domain' }}, so everything they type is encrypted. The admin page stays on your private network.
